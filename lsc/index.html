Emerging Leaders

Early-career professionals.
Real civic impact.

The Life Science Cares Philadelphia Emerging Leaders Committee is a newly forming group dedicated to activating early-career professionals in biotech, pharma, and healthcare around LSC's mission. We believe the next generation of life sciences leaders shouldn't wait until they're senior to start giving back — the time to build that habit is now.

The committee is in its founding phase, focused on building a community of engaged emerging professionals who show up for LSC events, take on volunteer roles, and help grow the organization's reach across the Philadelphia region.

If you're early in your career and want to connect with purpose-driven peers, develop civic leadership skills, and make a tangible difference in Philadelphia — this committee is being built for you.

Show up at events
Attend LSC Philadelphia's signature events — leadership lunches, service days, receptions — and become part of a community that cares about this city.
Take on volunteer roles
Help plan and execute the Emerging Leaders signature event. Represent early-career professionals within the broader LSC Philadelphia structure.
Build your network with purpose
Connect with peers across biotech, pharma, and healthcare who are building their careers and their communities at the same time.
